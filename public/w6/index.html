Collision Lab

Pick your game

Each one is a small playable version of a game like yours. You get five short missions: predict what will happen, break it on purpose, then see why. At the end you have a collision matrix for your own game.

1 PredictSay what you think will happen before you try it.
2 Break itUntick a rule or flip a switch and watch the game.
3 UnderstandOne idea, in one sentence, with the engine version.

Reference

Start from your game type, then tick which groups may touch which. Collision is mutual, so one tick covers both directions. Everything you do not tick is a check your game never runs.

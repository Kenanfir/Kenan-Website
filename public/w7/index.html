Game Development | Week 7 | Audio and juice

Sound Sources

Where to get sounds, the rule for each, and two small toys. Log every source you use in docs/audio-log.md.

1Make or get sounds

Start with the first one. Twelve sounds in fifteen minutes.

sfxr.me
Make sound effects in your browser. Press a preset (pickup, jump, hit, explosion), then export to download a WAV.
Free. You made it, so you can use it.
Kenney
Ready-made packs of interface sounds, impacts and more.
CC0: free to use, no credit needed.
Freesound
Real recordings: footsteps, doors, rain. You need a free account to download.
Read the licence on every sound. CC0 is free. CC-BY means you must credit the author. CC-BY-NC means no commercial use.
Suno
AI music. Ask for instrumental. Save every prompt.
Free plan: personal, non-commercial use. Paid plan: commercial use for songs made while subscribed. Terms change, so read the current ones before you publish.
Roblox audio
Roblox team: free sounds are in Studio's Toolbox, Creator Store tab, Audio. Uploading your own needs the rights to it.
Upload limits change. Check the upload window.

2Same sound, random pitch

The first button plays the exact same beep five times. The second changes the pitch a little each time, between 0.9 and 1.1.

Your browser plays a short beep. Turn your volume up a little.

3Hitstop: freeze on impact

Pick a hit weight, then press Swing. A light hit freezes about 2 frames, a heavy one about 8. The sound is a sequence: the hit lands on the first frozen frame, then the pause (the freeze), then the blow when the game resumes, then a whoosh as the enemy flies away. Heavier hits get bigger layers. Try Too long (20 frames) to see why games cap the freeze, and switch the sound off to hear what it adds.

Built for IMT01306618 Games Development, Universitas Ciputra, by Kenan Firmansyah. Hit sounds are layers from Kenney's Impact Sounds pack (CC0), plus a synthesised low boom. Licence notes were checked against each site in October 2026 and can change.
